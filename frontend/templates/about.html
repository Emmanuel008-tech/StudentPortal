About CampusPulse

Fostering academic transparency, student accountability, and connected collegiate life through intuitive digital tools.

Our Educational Mission

Transforming campus administration into a collaborative student partnership.

CampusPulse was designed around a fundamental principle: academic progress should never be obscured by administrative opacity. Students thrive when they have real-time visibility into their course standings, attendance metrics, and degree milestones.

By bridging the gap between student self-service and faculty oversight, our portal ensures every student is empowered with actionable insight before midterm deadlines or academic standing reviews arise.

The 75% Attendance Policy

Under university academic regulations, regular attendance in laboratory and lecture sessions is mandatory for degree certification:

≥ 75.0% Attendance ✓ Good Standing
< 75.0% Attendance ⚠ Standing Alert
0 Recorded Sessions ● No sessions yet

Students whose attendance falls into alert status are notified immediately and offered remedial advising through their academic counselors.
