Platform Capabilities & Features

Explore the comprehensive suite of tools built to streamline student life, course operations, and attendance governance.

Engineered for Clarity

Six foundational pillars of CampusPulse

From course enrollment to attendance dispute resolution, every feature is designed for speed and reliability.

Analytics

Attendance Intelligence

Instant calculations of attended versus total sessions with automatic teal Good Standing (≥75%) and coral Alert badges. Edge cases with zero sessions display a dedicated neutral slate state rather than false positives.

Academics

Self-Service Course Management

Browse active department catalogs, inspect credit breakdowns, syllabi, and assigned faculty instructors. Enroll in open courses or drop registered units with mandatory POST validation, CSRF protection, and confirmation modals.

Identity

Verified Student Identity

Each student record is securely linked via a OneToOne relationship to Django authentication. Profiles display standardized roll numbers, department affiliations, initials-based procedurally generated avatars, and editable academic bios.

Audit Trail

Granular Session Logbook

Students can view chronological records of every single class meeting, including date, course code, instructor remarks, and whether the attendance was recorded as Present, Late, or Absent.

Staff & Admin

Faculty & Staff Control Center

A dedicated, branded console for professors and registrars (`/accounts/staff/`) that aggregates registered student tallies, curriculum counts, and attendance entries, with one-click direct access to Django administrative tables.

Security

Account Recovery & Password Reset

Built on Django cryptographic token authentication. Students can safely initiate a recovery request via `/accounts/password-reset/`, receive time-limited recovery links, and re-establish account access securely.
